to matter before it's addressed. **Status**: `Open` / `Mitigated` (code

## Categories not yet assessed

These weren't in scope for this session's work and haven't been evaluated
at all — listed so they're not mistaken for "checked and fine":

- Tax obligations (VAT/sales tax handling, invoicing compliance for
  Zimbabwe and any other jurisdiction served).
- Employment law (not applicable while solo-founder-operated; revisit if
  hiring).
- Intellectual property audit of third-party libraries/fonts for
  commercial-use licensing (a standard MIT/Apache-licensed npm dependency
  tree is in use; no formal license audit has been performed).
- Trademark clearance for the "BusinessOS AI" name/brand.
