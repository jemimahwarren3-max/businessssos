## What the AI Assistant is

The AI Assistant is a feature that answers questions about your business
using a summary of your own data (sales, profit, expenses, top products and
customers, stock levels) and a third-party AI model (currently provided by
Groq). It's built to help you understand your numbers in plain language —
it is **not** a professional advisor of any kind.

## What it can't do

- It cannot take actions in your account — it can't create invoices, record
  sales, change prices, or modify any data. It only discusses the numbers
  it's given.
- It is not financial, tax, legal, or accounting advice. Nothing it says
  should be treated as a substitute for a qualified professional's opinion
  on a real business or financial decision.
- It only sees a 30-day summary of your business, not your complete history
  — it can be wrong, incomplete, or miss context a full review would catch.

## AI-generated information can be wrong

Like any AI system, the Assistant can produce answers that are incorrect,
outdated, or misleading, even when they sound confident. **Always verify
anything you plan to act on** against your actual records in the app (the
Reports and Dashboard pages, which pull directly from your data) rather than
relying solely on the Assistant's summary of it.

## What data it sees

See the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy#third-parties-we-share-data-with)
for the exact list of what's sent to Groq. In short: aggregated financial
figures, top product/customer names, and whatever you type — never your
customers' contact details or your payment information.
