## What this document covers

This explains what information BusinessOS AI collects, why, who it's shared
with, and what control you have over it. "You" means the person using the
Service; your business's customers whose records you enter into the Service
are a separate category, covered below.

## Information we collect

**Account information** — your email address, full name, and password
(Supabase Auth hashes your password; we never see or store it in plain
text).

**Business information** — your business name, type, currency, and the
phone number/address you optionally provide during setup.

**Business records you enter** — products, customers (name, phone, email,
address, notes if you add them), sales, invoices, quotes, expenses, and
suppliers. This is your data about your business and your customers, not
ours — see [Section 6 of the Terms of Service](/legal/terms#6-your-data-your-content).

**Payment information** — when you pay for a subscription, your email (and
phone number, if paying by mobile money) is sent to our payment processor,
Paynow. We never receive or store your card number or CVV — see
[Third parties](#third-parties-we-share-data-with) below.

**AI Assistant conversations** — questions you type to the AI Assistant, and
a summary of your business's numbers built to answer them. See
[AI_DISCLAIMER.md](/legal/ai-disclaimer) and the AI section below for exactly
what's included.

**Technical information** — standard web server logs (IP address, browser
type, request timestamps) generated automatically by hosting infrastructure.
We do not currently use any analytics or tracking tool (no Google Analytics,
Meta Pixel, PostHog, or similar) — this is a factual statement about the
codebase as it exists today, not a promise that will never change; if it
does, this document needs updating first.

**Cookies** — one essential cookie holds your encrypted session (so you stay
signed in). It's not a tracking cookie and isn't used for advertising — see
the [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies) for the full detail. Your
browser also loads Google Fonts' stylesheet and font files directly from
Google's servers, which means Google receives your IP address when a page
loads — see [Third parties](#third-parties-we-share-data-with).

## How we use this information

- To operate the Service: authenticate you, show your business's data, and
  process the actions you take (recording a sale, creating an invoice, etc.).
- To process payments via Paynow.
- To power the AI Assistant, by sending it a summary of your business's
  numbers and your typed question.
- To send transactional emails (sign-up confirmation, password reset) via
  Supabase Auth's email delivery.
- To maintain security (rate limiting, audit logs of account-level actions).

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Data storage and security

- All data is stored in Supabase's Postgres database with row-level security
  policies that enforce that one business can never read another business's
  data.
- Your session is stored in an encrypted, httpOnly cookie — not in browser
  local storage.
- `LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED`: confirm and state Supabase's at-rest encryption
  guarantees for this project's plan tier, and add any additional detail a
  reviewer wants about backup encryption/retention (see the open item
  below — no formal backup policy has been implemented yet).

## How long we keep your data

Your business data is kept for as long as your account exists. If you
delete your account (Settings → Account → Delete Account), every business
you solely own — and everything in it — is deleted immediately and
permanently, not just marked inactive. `LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED`: this
document should state a real backup-retention window once one is defined —
today, no separate backup-retention or purge policy exists beyond what
Supabase does by default, which we have not independently documented.

## Your rights

- **Access & export**: Settings → Export data gives you a complete copy of
  your business's data as a JSON file, at any time.
- **Deletion**: Settings → Account → Delete Account permanently deletes your
  business data and login. If you're not the sole owner of a business, this
  removes only your own access, not the business's data.
- **Correction**: you can edit or delete individual records (products,
  customers, etc.) directly in the app at any time.
